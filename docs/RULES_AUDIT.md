## Not stated on the official page

- Separate registration cutoff.
- Maximum team size.
- Pre-existing code policy.
- Required open-source license.
- KYC/payout mechanics.
- A mandatory live-money minimum (the page recommends a small mainnet demo, but owner approval remains required).

These are not assumed. The owner must confirm eligibility, legal terms and any form-only conditions before registering.
