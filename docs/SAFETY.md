# Safety and limitations

- Read-only and simulation-only by default.
- No private key ingestion, signing, approval, swap or broadcast code.
- `ACT` means evidence passes the safety policy; it is not financial advice or an executed trade.
- Every receipt requires owner approval.
- Missing values remain unknown and normally force INVESTIGATE.
- A derived reference price is labeled as derived, not represented as an official exchange quote.
- Fixtures are deterministic evaluation data, not live market claims.
- Tokenized securities may have jurisdiction, issuer, redemption and market-structure risks outside this engine.
